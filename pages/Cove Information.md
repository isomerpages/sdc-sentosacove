<p><a href="/files/Covesignage001.pdf" rel="noopener nofollow" target="_blank">Cove Signage Rules</a>
</p>
<p></p>
<p><strong>No Buggies &amp; PMDs</strong>
</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Footpaths on the foreshore are for residents to jog or to enjoy strolls
with their children,</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p>loved ones or pets.</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>For the safety of foot traffic, only bicycles are allowed. Cyclists should
not speed when</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p>riding on the footpaths.</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>All users should be socially responsible and share the use of the footpath.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>No fishing</strong>
</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Don’t break the rules. It is illegal to fish at any point at Sentosa and
those who flout the</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p>rules will be financially penalized.</p>
<p><strong>No swimming</strong>
</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>For their own safety, residents and guests are advised not to swim in
the open sea.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p>There are</p>
<p>i. strong currents;</p>
<p>ii. sharp rocks on the rock bund and on the sea floor;</p>
<p>iii. floating sea barriers (FSBs); and</p>
<p>iv. an active shipping channel beyond the FSBs</p>
<p><strong>Leash your dog and pick up after it</strong>
</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>All dogs should be leashed. This is to prevent dogs from inadvertently
attacking people</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p>and also to prevent them dashing out on to the roads.</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Be responsible for your pet and clean up after your dog. It is everyone’s
responsibility to</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p>maintain a clean and pleasant environment.</p>
<p><strong>Slippery when wet</strong>
</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>The footpaths have a pebble wash finishing which has a rough surface with
anti-slip</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p>properties but residents and guests are advised to walk with caution especially
when</p>
<p>the path is wet after rain. You don’t want to take an unnecessary tumble.</p>
