<p><strong>1. INTRODUCTION</strong>
</p>
<p>We at Sentosa Cove Resort Management Pte. Ltd. (“<strong>SCRM”</strong>)
are committed to protecting information under our control that identifies
you (“personal data”), including but not limited to your name, identification
numbers, contact information, photographs and video images. We also recognize
the importance of the personal data you have entrusted to us and believe
that it is our responsibility to properly manage, protect and process your
personal data. This Personal Data Protection Policy is designed to assist
you in understanding how we collect, use, disclose and/or process the personal
data you have provided to us, as well as to assist you in making an informed
decision before providing us with any of your personal data. If you, at
any time, have any queries on this policy or any other queries in relation
to how we may manage, protect and/or process your personal data, please
do not hesitate to contact our Data Protection Officer (the “<strong>DPO</strong>”)
at <a href="mailto:dataprotectionofficer@sedataprotectionofficer@sentosa.com.sgntosa.com.sg" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank"><u>dataprotectionofficer@sentosa.com.sg</u></a>.</p>
<p><strong>2. PURPOSES FOR COLLECTION, USE, DISCLOSURE AND PROCESSING OF PERSONAL DATA</strong>
</p>
<p>2.1 The personal data which we collect from you may be collected, used,
disclosed and/or processed for various purposes, depending on the circumstances
for which we may/will need to process your personal data, including:</p>
<p>(a) administration of services such as (i) responding to enquiries, requests,
compliments and complaints from you or people you have authorised, (ii)
processing applications from you or people you have authorised, (ii) processing
and updating you on the maintenance fund, maintenance fees contributions
and recover debts, and (iv) conducting data analytics for relevant government
authorities;</p>
<p>(b) updating you on any operational matters relating to Sentosa Cove and
Sentosa including but not limited to maintenance, infrastructure upgrades,
projects, transport, marine and waterway;</p>
<p>(c) informing and updating you on incidents and contacting you in crisis
situations;</p>
<p>(d) security, safety and risk management purposes such as (i) informing
you on safety, security and risk-related issues, (ii) preventing and detecting
fraud or other crimes, (iii) conducting audits and assessments, (iv) processing
and granting of access to limited areas (e.g. access cards, vehicular access,
watercraft access);</p>
<p>(e) service enhancements purposes such as conducting residents’ satisfaction
surveys to address concerns, develop new amenities and services, personalise
the services we offer, as well as improve customer service;</p>
<p>(f) sharing of privileges exclusive to residents where SCRM provides updates,
offers, invitations to events, and delivers advertising messages and materials
via digital publications or other publications, through voice calls with
your prior consent, or if otherwise permitted under Singapore laws and
regulations;</p>
<p>(g) evaluating, processing, and/or administering your request or application
for events programmes, or contests organised by SCRM or SCRM Partners,
handling any matters relating to your participation in the particular events,
programmes, or contests;</p>
<p>(h) complying with or as required by any applicable law, governmental
or regulatory requirements of any relevant jurisdiction, including meeting
the requirements to make disclosure under the requirements of any law binding
on SCRM or on SCRM’s related corporations (such as the Sentosa Development
Corporation) or associated companies and for the purposes of any guidelines
issued by regulatory or other authorities, whether in Singapore or elsewhere,
with which SCRM or SCRM’s related corporations or associated companies
is/are expected to comply; and</p>
<p>(i) any other purposes which we notify you of at the time of obtaining
your consent.</p>
<p>2.2 In order to conduct our operations more smoothly, SCRM may also be
disclosing the personal data you have provided to us to our third party
service providers, agents, employees and/or our affiliates or related corporations,
and/or other third parties whether sited in Singapore or outside of Singapore,
for one or more of the above stated Purposes. Such third party service
providers, agents, employees and/or affiliates or related corporations
and/or other third parties would be processing your personal data either
on SCRM’s behalf or otherwise, for one or more of the above stated Purposes.</p>
<p>2.3 Unless you have provided your consent for SCRM and/or SCRM Partners
to contact you for the purpose of providing and sending you marketing,
advertising and/or promotional information and materials via various modes
of communication such as (i) postal mail to your postal address(es) and/or
electronic transmission to your email address(es) (based on our records
that we have of your postal address(es) and email address(es) or any new
or amended postal or email address(es) that you may provide us with from
time to time) and (ii) via telephone/voice calls, SMS/MMS and/or facsimile
to your telephone number(s) (based on our records that we have of your
telephone number(s) or any new telephone number(s) that you may provide
us with from time to time), we will not send you any marketing messages.</p>
